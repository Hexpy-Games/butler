# Associative recall: evidence, decision, and gated plan

Design for owner approval, 2026-10-03. No production changes, builds, model calls, or data migration. Code references below are relative to `packages/butler-agent/rust/crates/butler-memory/src/cognition/`, at `09ffe9679`. Private data remain outside Git. Percentages are gold-source-episode hit@5 unless stated otherwise; a relevant alternative episode is not automatically gold.

**Recommendation:** first test conditional, evidence-aware reranking of the existing top 30, with the calling model retaining control of recall/read/refine. Aim for **at least 45% final-returned vague hit@5 and 50% vague-extra**, with no keyword/paraphrase regression. This is a target, not a demonstrated result. Then test a small, reversible write-side association pilot, especially session/activity links, before paying to change old data. Do not re-extract the archive, replace the embedding model, increase graph weights, or resurrect a full online generation-rebuild system for this work.

The original purpose remains sound: a partial recollection should activate related episodes, supply enough evidence for the model to recognise the event, and offer a one-step jump to the original conversation. Today the system mostly ranks independent source fragments. Making more fragments reachable and deciding which fragment the user means are separate problems. Neither more edges alone nor a stronger judge alone solves both.

### What ranking rewards

`memory_recall/selection/seeds.rs:160` bounds seeds to 16. `recall/expansion.rs:13` bounds traversal to three hops and 2,000 nodes. `recall/expansion/ppr.rs:108` weights navigation edges at 0.25 and generally damps reverse traversal by 0.5; identity/co-occurrence are navigation relations (`recall/expansion.rs:361`). Episode candidates are round-robin fused to 128 (`recall/ranking.rs:18`). These safeguards bound work, but a node's existence alone does not mean it enters the explored neighbourhood.

`memory_recall/selection/rank.rs:186` takes the maximum graph/lexical/context contribution over mentions, excluding supporting mentions and project nodes from graph relevance. `memory_recall/selection/rank.rs:352` supplies direct relevance as the maximum of raw lexical relevance and `1 - distance`; it always supplies `Some`, including zero for graph-only candidates. `recall/ranking.rs:78` combines reciprocal ranks, then `recall/ranking.rs:109` gives 55% to direct relevance, 35% to channel fusion and 10% to metadata. Graph relevance primarily improves a rank channel; it is not the 55% direct term. Exact raw matches are promoted afterward (`memory_recall/selection/rank.rs:108`). Session diversification changes equal-score ties, not all repeated-session results (`recall/ranking.rs:168`).

The supplied metric audit correctly identifies squared L2 on normalised vectors: cosine is `1 - d/2`. Changing only that formula loses 7–8 points overall because it also changes the relative scale against lexical relevance. `graph/recall/raw.rs:166` computes an IDF-weighted, length-saturated lexical score, not a calibrated probability or cosine. Both similarities and channel memberships are weak evidence of the particular event intended. Keep the mathematical correction separate from a jointly evaluated scoring change; do not describe the incorrect transform as cosine or ship its replacement alone.

Read-side identity contraction, alias/co-occurrence expansion, increased association weight, and graph-score promotion have already failed the supplied quality comparisons. Reported target-slice changes were +2.3/0 points for identity contraction (within noise), 0 to −5.8 for increased association weight, 0 to −4.4 for alias/co-occurrence, and −11.6 to −17.4 for graph-score promotion. They broaden or amplify ambiguous neighbourhoods without introducing enough event-specific evidence. The graph is not literally incapable of reaching anything; it currently contributes zero exclusive gold hits and cannot reliably deliver the intended associative behaviour.

## 2. Design space

### A. Write associations that have usable meaning

Keep source-local nodes. Make “shared identity” a reversible, scoped view over confirmed links, with source mentions still pointing to their original nodes. Candidate lookup can use name plus local activity/project context, Unicode-preserving aliases and the existing batched vector index. A cheap exact match can nominate a candidate; only stable source identity or a context-confirmed decision with both grounds may establish identity. A broad noun should usually become a topic membership, not an assertion that two distinct objects are identical.

Extend the existing binding decision, rather than adding another extractor. Store proposal/decision version, both source revisions and spans, applicable project/time scope, and active/revoked state. Apply/revoke under the consolidation lease; invalidating a supporting source invalidates its link. Do not rewrite node IDs, move evidence, or promote inferred links to explicit user statements. Do not take unrestricted transitive closure: one mistaken bridge must not silently identify an entire component. Preserve operator-only authority for existing-node canonicalisation; automatic navigation-link maintenance needs an explicit contract extension and tests for revocation.

For associations, favour entity → activity/session summary → source episode membership over pairwise cliques. An observed co-occurrence is evidence that things were discussed together, not that they are identical, liked, or causally related. Store source-backed window/activity membership and aggregate repeated independent support; downweight high-degree generic topics. A session can contain unrelated activities: split by evidence-backed activity when needed. Day summaries should be project-scoped and remain optional until session summaries prove insufficient. Dates and project membership are useful metadata, not enough semantic content on their own.

Start with deterministic membership and existing episode summaries. A generated session/activity summary may later describe what was being attempted, distinctive participants and outcomes, with citations to every included episode. An inferred umbrella topic must be labelled inferred. A summary hit returns underlying episode offers and original read/jump handles; it is not a new gold episode and does not replace canonical evidence.

Use these links first to propose candidates, with the path and its supporting excerpts available to the judge/caller. Do not assign a new path the same direct-relevance score as an exact source match. Test conjunctive support from the cue's known activity/entity/context and cap high-degree expansion within the existing candidate budget; merely sharing one generic topic must not boost every connected episode above direct hits. This is the write-side counterpart to E1, not another version of the failed graph-weight experiment.

**Expected gain:** unproven. Among the ten missing candidates, the co-occurrence ceiling adds four two-hop routes, at most 4/43 = 9.3 points if all became top-five hits; existing alias/session observations are overlapping, less restrictive opportunities. Identity contraction alone added only one reachable original miss across the earlier full miss analysis and no robust quality gain. A sensible pilot must earn at least two additional missing-candidate recoveries, rather than promise all ten. On vague-extra, require a separate measured ceiling; do not extrapolate the original ten's categories. Extra already has 56/69 golds in top 30, so immediate ranking headroom exceeds the case for archive-wide relinking.

**Risk and cost:** false identity and generic topic hubs can damage keyword/paraphrase precision. Membership is O(episodes + memberships), not O(nodes²). Deterministic grouping adds zero model tokens; binding needs only incremental context within existing calls where possible. A later generated synopsis costs approximately the tokens in the affected episode summaries plus 150–300 output tokens per changed activity; pilot at most 50 activities/100 identity pairs before estimating archive cost. This is a planning cap, not a measured token total. Store source references and changed summaries, avoid copying canonical text. Bound candidate expansion within existing budgets and measure every displaced direct candidate. No per-turn extraction, extra resident embedding model, idle scan, or timer. Recompute only changed groups during existing consolidation.

**Migration/generation:** new writes first; old nodes and vectors continue serving. Optional cheap backfill uses existing summaries and metadata, then separately approved, bounded identity decisions, checkpointed by source revision and last completed key. No blanket re-extraction. Components/memberships live within the active directory and use existing writer authority. A future full rebuild builds the same representation in a new offline directory and swaps the pointer after validation; no online shadow-generation lifecycle is needed.

### B. Conditional evidence-aware second-stage judge

Rank only the existing top 30 eligible episodes first. Preserve the base order on bypass, timeout, model unavailability, malformed output or insufficient evidence. The judge returns a validated permutation/relevance classification over supplied IDs; it cannot invent IDs, expand scope, claim truth, change model authority or decide whether the assistant may answer. The calling model owns recognition and any final uncertainty.

A confidence gate must predict ranking ambiguity, not answerability. Candidate diagnostics support the following initial signals:

| Signal / A1 slice | Vague | Vague-extra | Paraphrase | Keyword |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Median top-one lexical score | 0.114 | 0.106 | 0.152 | 0.334 |
| Median score gap between first two candidates | 0.0277 | 0.0292 | 0.0391 | 0.0421 |

For a reproducible starting experiment, let `L` be the base rank-one `l_score` and `M = abs(score[1] - score[2])` in the returned candidate order. Gate when `L < 0.20 AND M < 0.03`. Do not use query-style labels at runtime. On these traces that calls the judge on 20/43 vague, 32/69 extra, 23/67 paraphrase and 4/65 keyword queries: 79/244 (32.4%). It exposes 7/12/3/1 golds at ranks 6–30 respectively, but also 6/12/14/3 existing candidate-top-five successes. Therefore it is **not yet safe to ship**.

A stricter diagnostic gate (`L < 0.10`, `M < 0.05`) calls on 12/43, 25/69, 7/67, 0/65; only 5/13/0/0 rank-6–30 golds are recoverable. A looser gate (`L < 0.20`, `M < 0.05`) calls on 30/43, 45/69, 30/67, 8/65, exposing 11/18/3/1 recoverable golds. These alternatives bound the cost/recall tradeoff; they are exploratory, not fitted confidence probabilities. The strict gate's maximum original gain is only 5/43 even with a perfect judge. Do not promise the full unconditional gain from it.

Add an exact-evidence protection bit and separate raw-source matched-term coverage/IDF from alias scores before choosing the gate. Current `l_score` combines different lexical routes and lacks the exact-promotion flag; a high score or wide margin may still be confidently wrong. Check rank-five/six margin and channel disagreement as candidate features, but fit at most a small declared feature set on session-separated development data. Freeze thresholds before held-out evaluation. An exact-match-protection policy must be judged too; exact matching alone does not establish intended-event identity.

Show the judge the unchanged user cue, permitted known context, date/project metadata, a short episode summary, matched lexical terms, and one or two source-backed excerpts selected around the actual match/activity. Include negation, role, source revision and readable evidence handles. Do not just take the first 500 characters. In a blinded experiment compare summary-only against summary-plus-evidence with the same candidate set and token budget. The reported keyword regression is consistent with summaries omitting distinctive facts, but summary omission is not proven as its sole cause. Evidence windows are a retrieval aid; full source remains readable, and missing support must be declared rather than dropped to meet latency.

Start with guarded reciprocal-rank fusion, preserving protected exact-evidence candidates, versus a judge permutation. The supplied unconditional RRF experiment improved vague by 11.6 points and extra by 7.3, but lost 1.5 paraphrase and 6.1 keyword; overall stayed 68.0%, while hit@1 rose 41.7→47.4 and MRR .530→.571. Judge-alone extra 59.4% shows further potential, not a safe universal policy. A planning expectation of roughly **8–12 vague points and 5–10 extra points** is plausible only if gating retains the useful wins and evidence prevents losses. Gains from different experiments are not additive.

**Cost/privacy/offline:** one listwise request on gated recalls, zero extraction tokens, no migration. Budget about 200–350 input tokens per candidate plus 500–1,000 for instructions/cue: 6.5–11.5k input and 150–500 output tokens per call. Korean tokenisation and full evidence can change this; measure actual counts. At 32.4% gating, approximately 2.1–3.7k input and 49–162 output tokens per recall averaged over this benchmark. Monetary cost is those counts times the configured provider's input/output rates, not an assumed fixed dollar price. Dedicated API latency and configured-chat-model latency must be measured separately, including evidence fetch, network and cancellation; the reported 50 ms is not transferable. Initial incremental acceptance budget: gated p95 ≤500 ms, bounded by the existing operation deadline, not a longer timeout. Failure falls back to the complete base result.

Use the already selected/configured chat model by default, or an explicitly configured dedicated decision endpoint only after owner approval of that authority and data destination. Sending historical excerpts to another provider is a new disclosure. Never silently substitute a remote provider for offline/local mode. If no approved usable model exists, return base recall and let the caller read/refine. Do not load a new resident local model just for judging. The retrieval-planning spec retires separate planners/verifiers; a dedicated rank-only API needs a narrow normative exception before implementation. A same-model caller judging offers in its existing conversation is the conservative alternative, with more prompt tokens and possibly another turn round.

### C. Let the calling model use the context it actually has

Keep the cue intact. The caller can supply known project/session/time and activity phrases from the live conversation or cheap hot/feedback buffers. Separate known constraints from hypotheses: only known values become hard filters; hypotheses become alternate seed phrases or an explicit follow-up attempt. Preserve an unfiltered attempt when testing uncertain time/project guesses. Do not turn an abstract cue into invented biographical detail.

The tool already accepts `time`, project/session filters, seed phrases and bounded vector queries (`memory_recall/tool.rs:24`); results already carry evidence `read_args` and conversation locators. The immediate work is schema guidance and a measurable recall → read → refine example, not another hidden planner. `cursor` continues collected candidates; it does not explore a new neighbourhood. If needed later, add a versioned, scope-checked “related episodes from this evidence/session” operation, with explicit provenance, rather than smuggling IDs into the cue. Activity type initially belongs in semantic seed phrases; a hard activity filter requires a supported, evidence-backed index field.

Offer compact candidates with stable episode/revision, why-matched channels/path, date, enough recognition evidence, and a one-step original-conversation handle. Paginate full evidence without losing rank or latest-source validation. Use existing `partial`/coverage information for missing lanes; do not expose a fabricated confidence probability. Cap the initial experiment at one read/refine cycle; unresolved ambiguity goes back to the user for a useful detail, rather than a confident invented memory.

**Expected gain:** unknown on the generated set, which deliberately removes distinctive context. Same-session routes exist for 3/10 missing candidates, while 9/10 have same-day candidates; neither means the caller knows the right constraint. The already successful argument-guidance fix removes the case for expecting gains from rewriting every cue. Evaluate additional-context and cue-only arms separately, including ambiguous/unanswerable cues.

**Cost/risk:** no new extraction/storage or embedding residency; consumes existing caller-model tokens plus source-read/refine latency, potentially another roughly 3.3-second recall at the measured baseline. Wrong hard filters can remove gold entirely. No new writes or idle I/O, no archive migration. Existing handles pin the active generation/revision and become stale honestly after an offline pointer swap. Recent information stays in text buffers. Any vector use occurs only for an explicit recall operation under the embedding policy below.

### D. Improve representations only where a ceiling warrants it

“Embed episodes” is not a new proposal: `graph/vector_registration/units.rs:56` already embeds role-prefixed source chunks as episode units. Nodes use labels/aliases and structured claim content (`graph/vector_registration/units/nodes.rs:155`). The meaningful alternatives are a searchable episode-summary view, session/activity summaries, and evidence windows that retain surrounding intent/outcome. Test which unit gets a missing gold into top 30 before changing model size.

First try an offline lexical summary index over existing summaries plus current source retrieval. It costs no extraction/embedding calls and can reveal whether abstract activity wording is already stored. Ten missing golds have summaries, five with the answer substring, but this says nothing yet about cue-to-summary overlap. Keep source lexical retrieval for exact names and wording. For embeddings, compare existing source-window scores, node scores and optional summary representations on identical query/episode pools; do not merge incompatible embedding spaces or overwrite current vectors.

**Expected gain:** unmeasured; no defensible percentage yet. Re-embedding with the same native model failed, so execution format is not the demonstrated bottleneck. Tight positive similarities explain poor discrimination but do not prove a smaller or larger model fixes it. A model replacement belongs to a later batch evaluation of recall and cold-load/RSS cost. The existing vector lane's 16.6-point overall benefit argues against removing it now.

**Cost/risk:** summary lexical storage scales with summary bytes/postings; windows scale with source-window count and can duplicate near-identical candidates. Deduplicate at episode level without suppressing relevant evidence. Estimate vector storage as `units × dimensions × bytes_per_component`, plus measured index overhead; do not assert unknown unit counts or RSS savings. Summary generation adds tokens only if existing text fails; summary embeddings add one batched vector per unit. All updates are change-driven under consolidation, with zero idle scans. Keyword information loss is a major risk if summaries replace source units. Optional checkpointed side indexes can reuse old summaries; no compulsory re-extraction. A later model migration builds a new offline directory and validates it before pointer swap.

## 3. Embedding and generation contract shared by all options

Recent hot-cache and feedback-buffer content must remain cheap text reads. No automatic per-turn embedding, no keeping the embedding worker resident to repair benchmark cold latency, and no query-side extraction. Batch changed document/node/summary embeddings in consolidation and release the worker afterward. A, B and summary-lexical D can improve recall with zero new synchronous embedding work.

There is an unresolved distinction in the owner's principle: today's explicit vector recall embeds its query on demand and shares it between node and episode search; a completely cold worker misses the 750 ms lane deadline (`memory_recall/service.rs:269`). Document batches alone cannot compute the vector for an arbitrary future cue. If “never per turn” also forbids explicit-query inference, first-stage live recall must use lexical/graph/text context and optional previously available vectors; the reported warm vector gains then cannot be assumed. Recommend allowing on-demand inference only on explicit recall, batching its query variants together, with prompt unload and honest cold fallback, while banning routine per-turn work. If the owner chooses strict batch-only, rerun the entire recommended path with vectors unavailable. Do not silently keep a warm process or extend the deadline.

Incremental approved writes target the existing serving generation under consolidation ownership, source-revision checks and durable receipts. Future full rebuilds occur offline: new directory, complete source/count/vector-identity and recall validation, durable pointer swap, old directory retained for rollback. Do not copy an old canonical store over newer conversations. No shadow qualification state machine, online catch-up rebuild, automatic startup migration, or work in a refused legacy directory. Source-handle/cursor validity must be checked across a swap. This follows the separately reviewed generation simplification plan, not its retired rebuild APIs.

## 4. Cheapest experiments and stop conditions

These are approval candidates, **not experiments executed by this design task**. All run against fixed private artifacts or an explicitly disposable snapshot copy; only aggregates go to Git. No request-path or full-graph scans. Live model work, if later authorised, is separate from stub/replay product tests; any live test-cassette recording must use `openai/gpt-6-luna`.

### E0 — Freeze the question and measurement contract

Reconcile saved judge candidate IDs/permutations with A1 and response boundaries first. Recompute paired hit@1/5, MRR, candidate@30, final-returned@5, evidence readability and one-step conversation jumps. Preserve original strict gold and add separately adjudicated acceptable alternatives. Report envelope loss, candidate loss, vector availability, scope and generation independently. A compact-page change gets its own control, not credit assigned to this design.

Build a private real-cue set from the owner's reported 166 recall calls: locate calls by existing indexed turn/tool locators, read bounded surrounding user context, deduplicate repeated/debug calls, and separate user wording from model-written tool arguments. Do not scan multi-GB JSON stores on a request path. The 166 count is supplied, not independently verified here. A recall call is not automatically an answerable gold example. Have a blinded human identify the intended episode(s), ambiguity, and whether time/project/activity was actually available before retrieval. Preserve vague spoken references, code-switching and partial names; drop the no-distinctive-token generation rule. Hold out complete sessions/activities, with all variants of the same gold kept together; use a chronological held-out slice as a second check. Target at least 40 answerable real cues from at least 20 independent sessions plus 10 ambiguous/unanswerable cues; if fewer exist, report the shortage and gather prospectively, never pad with near-duplicates.

**Pass:** exact cohort/arm/rank reconciliation and evidence/jump validation; no gold/context leakage. **Stop:** missing judge artifacts prevent claiming a replay result; ambiguous gold prevents using that query for hit rate. Do not declare a statistically reliable product improvement from 43 correlated generated questions alone.

### E1 — Replay a conditional judge before any product change (first)

With saved candidate traces and judge outputs, apply the two-signal gates above, protected-evidence bypass, base/RRF/judge orderings, and unchanged top-30 membership in Python. No new model calls are necessary for this first screen. Report gated-call fractions, wins/losses by slice, and the maximum recoverable count. If saved judge outputs cannot be recovered, the current trace analysis provides only an oracle gate ceiling; request a separately authorised small evaluation, not fabricated replay scores.

Freeze one gate on development sessions, then evaluate held-out sessions; do not select thresholds on the final 43/69 outcomes. Next, only if promising, compare summary versus summary-plus-evidence using the same authorised judge and budget on a small balanced sample, then the fixed set. Include evidence fetching, provider token counts, timeout/cancellation and final response assembly. Recompute the gate after any ranker/compact-page change. Evaluate the configured chat model separately from the dedicated API; never transfer one model's wins to another.

**Pilot pass:** at least +4 net original-vague hits and +4 extra hits at candidate@5, no net keyword/paraphrase hit loss, no drop in their MRR, and every displaced prior hit reviewed. **Product target:** final returned ≥20/43 (46.5%, satisfying ≥45%) and ≥35/69 (50.7%), with keyword/paraphrase at least their same-run controls. Also require positive improvement on held-out real cues and report session-cluster bootstrap intervals; evidence/jump correctness 100% on returned gold. If the interval is inconclusive, remain experimental and gather more real cues.

**Resource pass:** bypass adds no provider call and ≤20 ms p95 in the perf tier; gated increment ≤500 ms p95, ≤12k input/500 output tokens per call, no extra model residency, no deadline increase, and average call fraction ≤40% on the realistic set. Measure cold and warm separately. **Stop:** keyword/paraphrase regress, the gate excludes too many recoverable golds to reach target, model cost/latency exceeds the declared budget, or evidence makes no improvement over summaries. Keep base recall; do not widen every query or tune until the held-out set becomes training data.

### E2 — Test known-context recall/read/refine

Using E0's real cues, compare unchanged cue-only recall against known-context fields, then one read/refine cycle. Initially simulate structured filtering over stored candidate metadata; this measures only ranking/filter ceilings, not retrieval of missing episodes. A later existing-harness run on a copy must measure new candidates and complete responses. Never inject gold dates/projects. Include wrong-hypothesis cases and unchanged-cue fallback. Reuse model decisions only where their inputs match exactly.

**Pass:** at least 3 additional correct final recalls per 40 answerable real cues over E1, no keyword/paraphrase loss, zero scope widening or unsupported answers in the ambiguous set, and at most one extra recall per affected query. Report full end-to-end latency rather than subtracting caller reasoning. **Stop:** gain appears only with oracle metadata, equivalent context was already supplied, or refinement mostly repeats the same candidates. Ship guidance only if it earns its extra work.

### E3 — Isolate write-side reachability from ranking

Build a small in-memory/private sidecar overlay from the existing snapshot's indexed evidence, aliases, session membership and summaries. First compare (a) confirmed identity links, (b) session/activity membership, (c) their combination. Alias equality only proposes pairs. Have a blinded reviewer confirm at most 100 pairs from source evidence, without query/gold labels; this makes an optimistic linking ceiling, not an automated extractor result. Separately test whether stored binding offers included those pairs. A later authorised extraction pilot must reproduce sufficient precision/candidate coverage before any product write path ships.

Use source-backed episode membership to simulate bounded traversal on the overlay. A later harness run on a disposable copy applies the links under the same scope/as-of/current-source checks and unchanged candidate budget, then reruns both base ranking and E1. Measure new gold candidates, direct-candidate displacement, degree distribution, edge precision, evidence validity and full results. A topology-only Python walk cannot certify real recall latency or ranking.

**Pass:** recover at least 2/10 currently absent original golds into top 30 and at least 3/69 extra golds into top 30; then earn ≥2/43 and ≥3/69 additional final top-five hits beyond E1 without keyword/paraphrase loss. All audited identity links must be correct (zero false identities in the pilot; report the small sample's uncertainty). Revocation must remove every dependent route. **Stop:** more edges but no new golds, only query/gold-informed labels help, unrelated generic hubs dominate, or any false identity survives. If the manually confirmed overlay fails, do not pay for automated linking/backfill. If identity-only fails and session membership passes, ship only membership.

### E4 — Cheap index comparison; model replacement last

Construct a private lexical index on existing episode summaries, followed by session-summary text assembled from existing summaries, without model calls. Compare union top-30 and final ranking to the same control with unchanged limits. This is the cheapest check for missing landing vocabulary. Then, only if lexical results or missing-candidate review support it, separately authorise batched summary/window embeddings on the small corpus. Compare units with the same model before comparing models. Preserve source lexical matches and report all candidate displacement.

**Pass:** at least +2 original and +3 extra golds in top 30, positive final top-five gain with E1, no keyword/paraphrase regression, and ≤10% additional measured retrieval p95 and peak working memory versus the same control; storage growth and batch token/inference costs must be reported and approved before backfill. **Stop:** no ceiling increase, gain disappears at fixed candidate budget, or only a full expensive re-extraction promises improvement. Do not fund a smaller/better embedding model experiment merely because the current distance formula was wrong.

## 6. Owner decisions and remaining unknowns

1. **Same configured chat model, or a dedicated rank-only decision endpoint?** Recommend same-model operation/caller first; permit the dedicated endpoint only if its measured cost/latency merits an explicit retrieval-authority exception and historical-evidence disclosure. Never silently switch providers.
2. **Allow on-demand query inference on explicit recall, or require strict batch-only embeddings even for queries?** Recommend the explicit-recall exception with worker release and cold fallback. Strict batch-only is coherent but requires vector-off acceptance measurements; warm benchmark gains would no longer describe live operation.
3. **Reversible navigation/membership links, or physical shared-node merges?** Recommend reversible source-backed links. Preserve operator-only canonical identity maintenance. Similar names and broad topics do not justify merges.
4. **New-data-only first, or an immediate optional backfill?** Recommend new-data-only until E3/E4 pass. Then offer a bounded existing-summary/membership backfill, with separately priced identity decisions; retain the option to skip it entirely.
5. **Accept a staged ≥45% vague / ≥50% extra target, or require ≥60% before any release?** Recommend the staged target with no keyword/paraphrase regression and real-cue confirmation. Candidate@30 ceilings make 60% theoretically possible but do not establish it; the current judge and missing-candidate evidence do not justify promising it.

Unknown until experiments: saved judge win/loss identities and provider cost; whether evidence cures summary regressions; a gate that preserves paraphrase successes; representativeness/answerability of the 166 real calls; acceptable alternate golds; whether cross-episode binding failures are proposal, decision or apply failures; scoped semantic precision of shared aliases; activity-summary incremental recall; cold/offline quality under the chosen query-embedding policy; resource costs and confidence intervals at realistic scale. No method can uniquely recover an event from a cue that contains no distinguishing information and no usable context; success then includes asking for the right additional detail.
