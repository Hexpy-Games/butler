City opens new riverside market
The market runs every Saturday from 9 a.m. with 40 stalls selling produce and crafts.
Parking is free for the first two hours. Bicycles can be left at the north gate.
Stall applications for the second month open next Monday on the city website.
Vendors must bring their own tables; tents are provided at the north and east rows.
A shuttle bus runs every 20 minutes from the central station between 8 a.m. and 2 p.m.
Food stalls will be grouped near the river terrace, with seating for about 200 people.
Recycling stations are placed at each entrance, and single-use plastic bags are not allowed.
The market committee meets monthly; minutes are published a week after each meeting.
Dogs on a leash are welcome in the outdoor rows but not in the covered hall.
Musicians can sign up for a 30-minute slot at the information desk on the day.
Organizers expect 3,000 visitors on the opening weekend.
Update: the opening is moved to Sunday because of rain.
