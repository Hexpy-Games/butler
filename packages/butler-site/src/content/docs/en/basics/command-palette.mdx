The command palette is a search window that finds conversations, projects, space groups, schedules and settings by name and opens them.

## Open and close

- Press <span data-mod-key><Kbd keys={["⌘", "K"]} label="Command K" /></span>. It opens even while you are typing in the composer.
- Click **Search** at the top of the sidebar.

To close the palette, press <Kbd keys={["Esc"]} label="Escape" /> or <span data-mod-key><Kbd keys={["⌘", "K"]} label="Command K" /></span>, or click the close button on the right or anywhere outside the palette.

## Find and open

<Steps>
  1. Press <span data-mod-key><Kbd keys={["⌘", "K"]} label="Command K" /></span> to open the palette.
  2. Enter a search term.
  3. Choose an item with <Kbd keys={["↑"]} label="Up arrow" /> and <Kbd keys={["↓"]} label="Down arrow" />, then press <Kbd keys={["Enter"]} />. You can also click the item.
</Steps>

## What you can search

Each result shows its kind or location. When you choose an item, what opens depends on its kind.

- **Chat**, **Project chat** — Opens that conversation.
- **Project** — Opens a new conversation in that project.
- **Space** — Opens the sidebar and expands that group to show it.
- **Schedule** — Opens the [schedule](/en/help/scheduled-tasks/) detail screen. The result also shows how often the schedule runs.
- **Settings** — Opens that [Settings](/en/help/settings/) screen. You can find **General**, **Appearance**, **Server**, **Models and access**, **Privacy and data**, **Diagnostics**, **System events** and **Archived**.

An item inside a group or project shows a location such as `Group › Project` in place of its kind.

## Search rules

- Search ignores case.
- If you enter several words separated by spaces, only items that contain every word appear.
- Conversations, projects and groups match on their title and on the names of the groups and projects that contain them. If you enter a project name, that project's conversations appear too.
- Items whose title equals the search term come first, then items that start with it, then the rest. Within the same rank, conversations and projects with recent activity come first.
- The palette shows up to 30 results.
- The part of a title that matches the search term is highlighted.
- If you open the palette without a search term, conversations and projects with recent activity come first.
- Archived conversations and projects, and conversations in archived projects, do not appear.

## Keyboard

- <Kbd keys={["↑"]} label="Up arrow" /> <Kbd keys={["↓"]} label="Down arrow" /> — Moves the selection.
- <Kbd keys={["Enter"]} /> — Opens the selected item.
- <Kbd keys={["Esc"]} label="Escape" /> — Closes the palette.
- <span data-mod-key><Kbd keys={["⌘", "K"]} label="Command K" /></span> — Opens or closes the palette.

## Notes

<Notice tone="info">
  Settings results appear when the search term matches the English section name. For example, `Appearance` finds **Appearance** and `Models` finds **Models and access**.
</Notice>

<Notice tone="info">
  While you are composing a Korean character, <span data-mod-key><Kbd keys={["⌘", "K"]} label="Command K" /></span> does not work. Finish typing the character, then press it again.
</Notice>

If the search fails, the **Could not search. Check your connection and try again.** message appears. Check your connection and click **Try again**.
