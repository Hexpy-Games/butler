The Library holds the files Butler delivered, the web pages it made, and the images, scraps and bookmarks you saved. Open it with **Library** in the sidebar.

## Open the Library

- Click **Library** in the sidebar.
- In the [file viewer](/en/help/basics/right-panel/#view-files), click **Open in Library** to open the Library's **Documents** searched for that file's name.

## What the Library holds

The main screen shows **Scraps**, **Documents**, **Bookmarks** and **Outputs** in that order. Each area shows its item count and recent items. Click **View all** to open the full list of that kind.

- **Scraps** — Parts of pages you saved from the browser, and images and answer cards you saved with **Save to library**. You can sort them into groups.
- **Documents** — Files Butler delivered and editable documents.
- **Bookmarks** — Browser bookmarks. You can sort them into folders.
- **Outputs** — Web pages Butler made.

## Find items

<Steps>
  1. Enter a search term in **Search the library** at the top.
  2. Choose **All**, **Scraps**, **Documents**, **Bookmarks** or **Outputs** to narrow the kind.
  3. If needed, filter the list with the **Tags**, **Sites**, **Date** and **Sort** menus.
</Steps>

Filters you apply appear below the search box. Click **Clear all** to remove them all at once.

## What gets saved to the Library

| Item | Saved under | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Files Butler delivered in a reply | **Documents** | Automatically |
| Web pages Butler made | **Outputs** | Automatically |
| Changed files | **Documents** | **Save to library** in the changed file's menu |
| Images Butler made | **Scraps** | **Save to library** on the image |
| Answer cards | **Scraps** | **Save to library** in the card's menu |

Images Butler made are not saved automatically. To keep one, click **Save to library** on the [image card](/en/help/basics/images/#image-card) or in the image viewer.

## Versions

When Butler delivers the same file again in the same conversation, no new item is created. The file is added to that document as a new version.

- Each document keeps up to its 20 most recent versions. Older versions are removed.
- If the content is the same as the previous version, no new version is added.
- Choose an earlier version under **Version** in the file viewer.

## Open files

Click an item to open it in the [file viewer](/en/help/basics/right-panel/#view-files) inside the Library. When you close the file, you return to the list with its filters and scroll position unchanged.

- A document Butler made is editable: click the text and type. It saves as you go; **Versions** shows earlier versions and restores one.
- A scrap opens on a detail screen of what you saved.

The item's **More** menu has these actions.

- **Rename** — Changes the item's name.
- **Move to group…** / **Move to folder…** — Moves a scrap into a group or a bookmark into a folder.
- **Edit tags…** — Adds or removes tags.
- **Attach to chat** — Attaches the item to a conversation.
- **Go to chat** — Opens the conversation where the file, document or web page was made.
- **Open source** — Opens the page or conversation a scrap or bookmark came from.
- **Delete** — Removes the item from the Library.

## Notes

<Notice tone="warning">
  **Delete** in the item menu removes the item right away, without asking. Deleted items can't be restored.
</Notice>

<Notice tone="info">
  Butler delivers up to 12 files at a time, each up to 10 MB.
</Notice>
