The sidebar is where you find and organize conversations and projects. The list of conversations, projects and groups in the sidebar is called the **Space**. Create groups to arrange it the way you want.

## Sidebar layout

The sidebar has these items, from the top.

- **New chat** — Starts a new conversation.
- **Search** — Opens the [command palette](/en/help/basics/command-palette/). You can also press <span data-mod-key><Kbd keys={["⌘", "K"]} label="Command K" /></span>.
- **Schedules** — Opens the list of [schedules](/en/help/scheduled-tasks/).
- **Browser** — Opens the [browser](/en/help/basics/browser/). Only in the desktop app.
- **Library** — Opens the [library](/en/help/basics/library/).
- **Favorites** — Shows up to two pinned conversations and projects. If there are more, click **Show all favorites**.
- **All**, **Recent**, **Running** — Tabs that change how the list is shown.
- **Space** — Shows conversations, projects and groups as a tree.
- **Settings** — Sits at the bottom of the sidebar.

**Chats**, at the top of the **All** tab, is the default conversation and is always there. You cannot move, rename, archive or pin it. Its menu has only **Reference in message** and **Clear chat**.

### Clear chat

Send the content of **Chats** to the archive and start fresh on an empty screen. Instructions and chat memory stay.

<Steps>
  1. On the **All** tab, open the menu of the **Chats** row.
  2. Choose **Clear chat**.
  3. In the confirmation dialog, click **Clear**.
</Steps>

The earlier content is archived with the title `Chats · date`, and you can restore it from [**Archives**](/en/help/settings/#archives). Click the **Manage memory ›** link in the dialog to open **Settings → Memory**. You cannot choose **Clear chat** while work, an approval request or a subtask is in progress.

### Switch list views

- **All** — Shows the group and project structure as a tree. Dragging to organize works only on this tab. Unfinished subtasks and project tasks also appear only on this tab.
- **Recent** — Lists every conversation by last activity. Each conversation shows its location and the time of its last activity.
- **Running** — Shows only conversations that are working now or need your attention. A **Work status** list follows below, where you can check each work stage and the number of steps done.

When the list is long, a **More** item appears at the end. The number in parentheses is how many items are not shown yet.

## Create a group

A group is a folder that holds conversations and projects.

<Steps>
  1. On the **All** tab, open the **Space menu** next to the **Space** heading and choose **New group**.
  2. In the **Create group** dialog, enter a **Group name**.
  3. Click **Save**.
</Steps>

To create a group inside a group or project, choose **Create subgroup** from that row's menu.

When you drag a conversation onto another conversation, **Group together** appears. Drop it there and enter a group name to create a group that holds both conversations. You can group only conversations in the same project, or conversations that are both outside a project.

When **Smart groups** is on, Butler automatically organizes new conversations into groups by topic. Conversations you moved yourself stay where you put them. It is on by default. Turn it off in **Sidebar** under [**Settings → General**](/en/help/settings/#general).

## Move and reorder items

### Drag to move

<Steps>
  1. Open the **All** tab.
  2. Drag a conversation, project or group row and drop it where you want it.
     - Drop it above or below another row to change the order.
     - Drop it on a group or project to move it inside.
     - Drop it on the **Move to space root** area at the end of the list to move it to the top level.
</Steps>

While you drag, move the pointer to the top or bottom edge of the list and the list scrolls automatically.

### Move from the menu

<Steps>
  1. From the row's menu, choose **Move…**.
  2. In the **Move to** dialog, choose a group or project. Enter a name in the field to find a location, or choose **Space root**.
</Steps>

### Moves that change the project

When you move a conversation to another project or out of a project, the **Change this conversation's workspace?** dialog opens. Click **Move** to keep the conversation history and existing files as they are. From then on, work uses the work folder and project knowledge of the new location.

## Row menu

Hover over a row to show the menu button (⋯). You can also right-click the row or press and hold it to open the same menu.

- **Reference in message** — Adds this conversation to the composer as a reference. You can also drag the conversation row into the composer.
- **New chat** — On group and project rows. Starts a new conversation inside that group or project.
- **Project dashboard** — Only on project rows.
- **Rename** — Renames the conversation, project or group.
- **Move…** — Opens the dialog for choosing a location.
- **Create subgroup** — On group and project rows.
- **Pin** / **Unpin** — On conversation and project rows.
- **Archive** — Hides the conversation or project from the list and sends it to the archive. Groups do not have it.
- **Delete project** — Only on project rows.
- **Dissolve group** — Removes only the group and moves the items inside it to where the group was.

## Subtasks in the sidebar

When Butler splits work into [subtasks](/en/help/basics/conversation/#subtasks), the **All** tab shows each unfinished subtask on its own line under its conversation. Finished subtasks leave the list. They do not appear on the **Recent** and **Running** tabs or in **Favorites**.

Depending on its state, the right side of the line shows one of these.

- Step progress — While the subtask works, the current step appears, such as `3/5`.
- **Resume** — Restarts a paused subtask.
- Warning icon — The subtask is waiting for an approval.

Click the line, or hover over it and click **View progress details**, to open the parent conversation with the work log.

On a conversation row, the right side shows a progress indicator while it works, a **Needs attention** icon when something needs you, and a dot when it has an unread reply.

## Tasks in projects

On the **All** tab, expand a project to see its [task](/en/help/projects/#board) groups above its conversations.

- **Backlog** — Tasks not in a plan.
- Plan name — One group for each plan.

A group lists only tasks that have a conversation or are assigned to **Me**. A task row shows its assignee. Click it to open the task's conversation. If the task has no conversation yet, one is created when you first open it. A conversation that worked on only one task leaves the project's conversation list and moves under that task's row.

- Click **New Epic** (+) on a group heading to create a task in that group.
- From the menu (⋯) on a group heading, open **Plan overview**. On a plan group, choose **Edit** to edit the plan.
- Drag a task within its group to reorder it. Only your own sidebar order changes.
- Drop a task on another group's heading to move it to that plan.

A task row that has a conversation uses the same menu as a conversation row.

## Favorites

Pin the conversations and projects you open often to **Favorites** at the top of the sidebar.

- From the row menu, choose **Pin**.
- For a conversation, you can also hover over the icon on the left of the row and click it to pin or unpin.

You cannot pin groups to favorites.

## Archive and restore

<Steps>
  1. From the row menu, choose **Archive**. For an open conversation, you can also choose **Archive** from the menu (⋯) on the right of the title bar.
  2. To see archived items, choose **Archive** from the **Space menu** next to the **Space** heading. When there are archived items, it shows as **Archive (N)**. The [**Archives**](/en/help/settings/#archives) screen in Settings opens.
  3. Click **Unarchive** to return an item to the sidebar list, or click **Delete** to delete it permanently after you confirm.
</Steps>

When you archive a project, its conversations are hidden from the list too.

## Hide and resize the sidebar

- Use the **Hide sidebar** / **Show sidebar** button to collapse and expand the sidebar.
- Drag the right edge of the sidebar to change its width. If you drag it narrower than the minimum width, the sidebar hides.
- With keyboard focus on the edge, you can also adjust the width with <Kbd keys={["←"]} label="Left arrow" /> and <Kbd keys={["→"]} label="Right arrow" />.
- When the window gets narrow, the sidebar and the right panel close automatically. When you widen the window again, they return to their previous state.

## Notes

<Notice tone="warning">
  **Dissolve group** and moves that do not change the project apply immediately, without confirmation. Conversations and projects removed with **Delete** in the archive cannot be restored. Deleting a project does not delete its local folder.
</Notice>

<Notice tone="info">
  You cannot move a group or project into another project. You cannot put other items inside a conversation. A conversation with work in progress can move to another project after the work finishes.
</Notice>
